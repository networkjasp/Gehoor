How we choose the right hearing aid with you

1. Your hearing

We start with a full hearing test, so the technology matches your type and degree of hearing loss.

2. Your lifestyle

Busy restaurants, church, the bushveld, the office: we ask where you most want to hear better.

3. Your budget

We explain the options at each price level and what your medical aid will cover.

4. A trial at home

Wear them in your real life before you decide.

5. Fitting & fine-tuning

We programme them to your hearing and fine-tune them over the first weeks. Bring a family member: a familiar voice helps.

6. Lifelong aftercare

Annual check-ups, cleaning and repairs, with courier fees waived on warranty repairs.

Myths about hearing aids

Myth: Smaller always costs more

Price depends mostly on the technology inside, not on size. A small aid can be basic, and a larger one can be top of the range.

Myth: Hearing aids restore normal hearing

They make sounds and speech much clearer, but they don't make hearing perfect. Realistic expectations make for happier wearers.

Myth: Batteries are a constant hassle

Most new hearing aids are rechargeable with a lithium-ion battery. Pop them in the charger at night, the same as your phone.

Myth: Once fitted, they look after themselves

Hearing aids should be serviced at least once a year to keep them working at their best.

Myth: I'll only need them for special occasions

Your brain adapts best when you wear them every day, all day. Part-time wearing makes it harder to get used to them.
