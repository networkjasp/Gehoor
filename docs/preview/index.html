Your first visit

What to expect, step by step

  1. 1
    A conversation. We ask about your hearing, health and the situations you find hardest.
  2. 2
    A look in your ears. We check for wax or anything blocking sound.
  3. 3
    The hearing test. Painless. You listen in a soundproof booth and press a button when you hear a tone.
  4. 4
    Your results, explained. You leave with clear options and a written report, with no pressure to buy.

Bring along

  • Your ID and medical aid card
  • A doctor's referral, if you have one
  • Your current hearing aids
  • A list of your medication
  • A family member: a familiar voice helps when we fit hearing aids

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can make an appointment with us directly. Some medical aids ask for a referral before they pay for hearing aids, and we'll tell you if yours does.

Does my medical aid pay for a hearing test?

Most medical aids cover a diagnostic hearing test. We submit the claim for you, and help with motivations and quotes for hearing aids at no charge.

Can I try hearing aids before I buy?

Yes. We offer a trial period so you can wear them at home, at work and at church before you decide.

Which hearing aid brands do you fit?

Rexton, Unitron, Widex and Signia, plus Cochlear and Baha implant solutions. We're independent, so we recommend what suits you.

Ready to hear better?

Send us a message and our friendly reception team will find a time that suits you.
