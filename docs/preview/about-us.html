Caring for the Moot's hearing since 1999

What a privilege it has been to serve the people of the Moot and beyond for more than 27 years. We're an independent practice: we recommend what's right for you, not what a manufacturer wants sold.

Serving the community

We provide newborn hearing screening at Life Eugene Marais Hospital, and work with local schools, care homes and community organisations to make hearing care easier to get.

Through donation programmes run with manufacturers, we've helped people who could not otherwise afford hearing aids. Ask us about current programmes.
