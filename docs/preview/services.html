Gehoortoetse

Diagnostic hearing tests

A full hearing assessment takes about an hour in our soundproof booth. It tells us the type and degree of any hearing loss, and whether there's an underlying medical issue that needs a referral.

  • Pure-tone and speech testing
  • Middle-ear (tympanometry) testing
  • Results explained the same day, with a written report
  • Reports for your doctor, ENT, employer or medical aid

Free hearing check

Do I need a hearing test?

Answer these 8 questions honestly. If you answer "yes" to three or more, a hearing test is a good idea. Hearing loss usually creeps up slowly, and the people around you often notice it first.

1. Do you often ask people to repeat themselves?
2. Is it hard to follow a conversation in a restaurant or at church?
3. Do family members say the TV or radio is too loud?
4. Do people seem to mumble or speak softly?
5. Is it hard to hear on the phone?
6. Do you struggle to hear women's or children's voices?
7. Do you hear ringing, buzzing or hissing in your ears?
8. Do you avoid social situations because of your hearing?

Answer the questions to see your result.

This isn't a diagnosis. It helps you decide whether to make an appointment.

Tinnitus & hiperakusis

Tinnitus and hyperacusis treatment

Tinnitus is ringing, buzzing or hissing that no one else can hear. Hyperacusis makes everyday sounds feel too loud or even painful. Both are common, and you don't have to "just live with it". There's no one-size-fits-all cure, but most people get real relief with the right plan.

  1. 1
    Full assessment: a hearing test, tinnitus pitch and loudness matching, and a sound-tolerance check.
  2. 2
    Understanding it: knowing what causes your tinnitus takes away much of the fear and stress.
  3. 3
    Sound therapy: hearing aids with built-in tinnitus programmes, sound generators or apps.
  4. 4
    Follow-up: regular check-ins, and a referral to an ENT or psychologist when needed.

Tinnitus self-check

1. Do you hear ringing, buzzing or hissing most days?
2. Does it bother you when you try to sleep?
3. Does it make it hard to concentrate?
4. Do everyday sounds feel too loud or painful?
5. Has it lasted longer than 3 months?

Answer the questions to see your result.
