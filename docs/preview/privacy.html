Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

Dr René Hornby Inc., trading as Gehoor in die Moot (registration 2019/150857/21, practice number PR 8212155), is the "responsible party" for personal information collected through this website and at our practices at 536 18th Avenue, Rietfontein and 663 5th Avenue, Les Marais, Pretoria.

2. What we collect

Through this website

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), preferred practice and language, the topic and your message.
  • Technical information: your IP address is used briefly to block spam and abuse, and is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Analytics, only if you accept: anonymous information about the pages you visit, your device type and how you arrived at the site.

When you become a patient

We collect the health, contact, identity and medical aid information needed to assess and treat you. Health information is special personal information under POPIA and is processed only for your care, as allowed by section 32 of POPIA and the HPCSA's rules on confidentiality.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your message and arrange appointments.
  • To provide hearing care, and to submit claims, quotes and motivations to your medical aid.
  • To keep this website secure and working, and, with your consent, to understand how it is used so we can improve it.

We never sell your information, and we don't use it for marketing without your consent.

4. Who we share it with

  • Your medical aid scheme, referring doctor or specialist, when needed for your care and with your knowledge.
  • Hearing aid manufacturers, for orders, warranties and repairs.
  • Service providers who help us run the practice and this website (hosting, email, and Google Analytics if you accept). They may only use the information on our instructions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some providers, such as Google, may store information outside South Africa. When this happens we rely on section 72 of POPIA: the recipient must be bound by adequate data protection rules or contract terms.

6. How long we keep it

Messages sent through the website are deleted from the website system within 12 months, or sooner once they've been dealt with. Patient records are kept for as long as the HPCSA and South African law require.

7. How we protect it

The website runs only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The staff portal is password-protected, uses limited login attempts and automatic logout, and keeps website messages away from public access. In the practice, records are kept securely, and staff are bound by confidentiality.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask whether we hold information about you, and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is wrong, out of date or no longer needed;
  • object to how we use your information, or withdraw your consent;
  • complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Requests for records can also be made under our PAIA manual, which is available on request.
