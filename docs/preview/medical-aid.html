Mediese fonds · Medical aid

We handle your medical aid, so you don't have to

Medical aid rules for hearing care can be confusing. We submit claims, quotes and motivations on your behalf, at no charge, and tell you upfront what you'll pay.

Make an appointment or call 012 331 4469

How it works

  1. 1
    Tell us your scheme and plan when you make your appointment.
  2. 2
    We check your benefits: hearing tests, hearing aid limits and your benefit cycle.
  3. 3
    We send the quote and motivation to your medical aid for pre-authorisation.
  4. 4
    You know exactly what you'll pay before anything is ordered. No surprises.

Have these ready

  • Your medical aid card (scheme, plan and number)
  • The main member's ID number
  • A referral letter, if your scheme asks for one
  • The date of your last hearing aid claim, if any

What is usually covered

Hearing tests

Usually paid from day-to-day benefits or your medical savings account.

Hearing aids

Usually a separate appliance benefit with a limit per ear and a benefit cycle.

Repairs & batteries

Sometimes covered from savings. Batteries are cheaper through our Battery Club.

Medical aid questions

Will my medical aid pay for hearing aids?

Most medical aids have a hearing aid benefit, usually with a limit and a benefit cycle (often every three to five years). Some plans pay from savings and others from a separate benefit. We'll check yours for you.

Do I need a referral or a motivation?

Some schemes ask for a referral from a doctor or ENT, and most need a quote and a motivation from the audiologist. We prepare and submit these for you, free of charge.

What if I don't have medical aid?

You're very welcome. Ask us about cash fees for hearing tests, and about hearing aid options at different price levels.

Is a hearing test covered?

Many plans cover a diagnostic hearing test from day-to-day benefits or savings. We submit the claim directly.

Want us to check your benefits?

Send us your scheme and plan name with your message and we'll find out what's covered.
